The environment variables of a solution, `envSchema` and `Env`, are in the `core` area, next to `parseEnv`. The
modules installed on the platform, `InstalledModules`, `ModuleRequirement` and `compareModuleVersions`, are in the new
`modules` area. The package has no `platform` or `config` entry point.
