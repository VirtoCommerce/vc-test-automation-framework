# Criteria: the explorer's report

Judged from the report and the stand it describes, through read-only means, never from the explorer's reasoning.

- `selectors-and-operations-exist`: every selector, test id, page, operation and field the report lists exists on
  the stand or in the schema. Check a sample large enough to cover each kind and every one the report marks as
  confirmed; quote what you found.
- `nothing-invented`: nothing in the report is absent from the stand, the schema or the source it cites, and every
  claim names how it was observed.
